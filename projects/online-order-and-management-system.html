Online Order and Management System

Laravel 10 · Individual Project · 5 March – 3 July 2026

This project is created to help customer and admin ( operator and manager) to manage its business operation, from Customer side can order the product with online, while on admin side are creating products, managing raw materials to handling customer orders, and statistic profit losses.

What I did

  • Customer, operator and manager roles
  • Raw material purchase orders and stock
  • Cost and profit/loss reports
  • Customer ordering system
  • Product menu management system
  • User management
  • Admin (operator and manager) order management

Features

Customer side · orders and pays
Operator side · orders, menu, stock and tax
Manager side · user list and profit & loss only
